Who We Are

Greensurface Projects, launched in 2016, is a South African engineering company committed to environmental preservation and best-in-class engineering. We manage projects from concept to commissioning, ensuring success through advanced process engineering and project management.

Vision and Mission

We aim to lay strong foundations for today and future generations while building a culture of operational excellence.

Ethics

We prioritize environmental and individual health amidst growing development, combining market expertise with a focus on client growth and conversions.

What Sets Us Apart

We treat clients as partners, measuring our success by theirs. Our young, innovative team takes risks to exceed expectations, delivering showcase projects that reflect our culture and commitment.

Our Success

We define success through happy clients, top engineering, and innovation. By understanding your goals, challenges, and opportunities, we’ve completed numerous successful projects.

Our Team

Our team comprises the best talent in the industry, blending experience with fresh perspectives. Committed to growth, our skilled resources are the foundation of Greensurface Projects.

Gauteng Water: Water and Engineering Solutions

Alleviating water challenges across Gauteng and Southern Africa.

Water Consultants

Our expert water consultants at Gauteng Water provide tailored strategies to address your water management needs. We assess, plan, and implement solutions that ensure sustainable water usage, helping you navigate challenges and achieve long-term efficiency.

Water Storage Solutions

Gauteng Water offers innovative water storage solutions designed for reliability and sustainability. From tanks to reservoirs, our systems are built to secure water supply for communities, farms, and industries, even in challenging conditions.

Municipal Water Backup Systems

We provide robust municipal water backup systems to ensure uninterrupted water supply during outages. Gauteng Water’s solutions are designed to support municipalities with reliable, efficient systems that minimize disruptions and maintain service quality.

Pump Solutions and Accessories

Gauteng Water delivers high-performance pump solutions and accessories to meet diverse water management needs. Our pumps are engineered for efficiency, durability, and sustainability, ensuring optimal water flow for any application.

Water and Sanitation Accessories

Our water and sanitation accessories at Gauteng Water are designed to enhance system performance and hygiene. From filtration units to sanitation fittings, we provide solutions that ensure clean, safe water for all users.

Irrigation Systems

Gauteng Water’s irrigation systems are tailored to maximize agricultural productivity while conserving water. We design and install efficient systems that deliver precise water distribution, supporting sustainable farming practices.

Our Clients

We’re proud to partner with a diverse range of clients across South Africa, delivering sustainable engineering and water solutions that make a difference.
